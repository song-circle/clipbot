# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 19, 2024**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ClipBot ("we", "our", or "us") handles your information.

## 2. Information We Collect

ClipBot may collect the following information:
- **OAuth Tokens**: For authenticating with social media platforms (YouTube, TikTok, etc.)
- **Video Metadata**: Information about videos you upload
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with the application

## 3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:
- Authenticate with third-party platforms
- Upload videos on your behalf
- Improve our service

## 4. Data Storage

- All credentials are stored locally on your device
- We do not store your data on external servers
- OAuth tokens are saved in local configuration files

## 5. Third-Party Services

ClipBot integrates with:
- **YouTube API Services** - Subject to [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **TikTok API** - Subject to [TikTok Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- **Pexels API** - Subject to [Pexels Privacy Policy](https://www.pexels.com/privacy-policy/)
- **Pixabay API** - Subject to [Pixabay Privacy Policy](https://pixabay.com/service/privacy/)

## 6. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your information:
- Local storage only
- No transmission to external servers
- Credentials stored in gitignore'd files

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your data
- Delete your data
- Revoke API access at any time

## 8. Children's Privacy

ClipBot is not intended for users under 13 years of age.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

## 10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, please contact through GitHub Issues.
